# Phonics recording script

Implementation text frozen for teacher review, 2026-09-23. **Not teacher-approved.** Do not record until the checklist is signed. No audio was generated, edited, deleted, or replaced in this improvement.

This is the proposed final recording specification. Slashes are production notation, not words to read aloud. Use a Grade 1 reading teacher to model each sound. Keep stop consonants brief; never append a strong schwa or say a letter name in a sound file. Keep word files to the word only: no spelling, target letter, praise, or answer explanation. Use the same agreed classroom accent throughout.

The app currently plays local letter-name and whole-word files. No existing clip is accepted as an isolated sound recording; all Hear Sound buttons are withheld. Name, sound, and word recordings are separate. Reuse exactly matching recordings across lessons and games. C and K share one /k/ recording; I and P share the single Pig word file. Do not make duplicate copies for each activity.

Short O needs explicit accent approval: displayed /ɒ/ is the British-style short-O notation; many American speakers use /ɑ/ in octopus. Do not record long /oʊ/. Classroom /y/ means the consonant /j/ in yarn, and /r/ means the English consonant /ɹ/, not a trilled r. /sh/ means /ʃ/. Qu /kw/ and X /ks/ each represent **two** sounds; do not describe either as one phoneme.

## Dynamic Quick Check prompts

These remain text plus the independently recorded word cue. Do not record a combinatorial set of every word/letter prompt. If spoken prompts are added later, use generic instructions above, then the word file. Spelling questions must be labeled as spelling or letter identification. Never insert the correct letter into a sound-question recording. Human listening review must confirm word clips contain no answer hints.
